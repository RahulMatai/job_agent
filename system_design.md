# System Design — AI Job Alert Agent

## Problem Statement
Job seekers miss good opportunities because they check job sites manually,
hours or days after posting. Early applicants have significantly higher
chances of getting noticed.

## User Story
As a job seeker, I want to be alerted within hours of a relevant job being
posted so that I can apply before hundreds of others do.

## Target User
- Rahul Matai
- Roles: Backend Engineer, Software Developer, ML/AI Engineer, Agentic AI
- Location: India + Ireland
- Experience: 4+ years Python

## Data Sources
| Source | Method | Coverage |
|--------|--------|----------|
| LinkedIn | RSS via RSS.app | High |
| Indeed India | Native RSS | High |
| Indeed Ireland | Native RSS | Medium |
| Naukri | Selenium scraping | High |
